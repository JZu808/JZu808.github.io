## Why Engineering?
<img width="300px" class="rounded float-start pe-4" src="..\img\metal gear rising.jpg">

I enjoy watching a lot of sci-fi shows and movies like Iron Man, Gundam, AKIRA and playing sci-fi video games like Call of Duty Black Ops 2, Titanfall, Metal Gear Rising and enjoyed seeing futuristic technology and devices being used in action. I chose to study engineering because I wanted to contribute to developing these futuristic devices and bring fantasy to reality.



## Engineering is not for the Weak

As I take on my senior year of studying Computer Engineering at UH Manoa, engineering courses has humbled me and stressed me out many times. We are supposed to fail and learn from mistakes but what makes it stressful is the fact that we are graded on our failures and nothing can make it up. Even though I have almost failed a few courses or did not live up to my potential, I'm in the deep end of my undergrad career and can't give up now and I still believe that I can be a good engineer. 

## Potential Career Change

Since the summer of 2025, I have picked up a few hobbies such as drawing and playing guitar. I enjoyed these hobbies to a point where I felt as if I was more of a artist or musician rather than an engineer. When I graduate, I could put more time into honing my art and music skills and make a career off of that, even though I would have a Computer Engineering degree by then.

I didn't want the skills I learned in college to go to waste but I also wanted to have a career as an artist. I am still looking for a career that can combine these artistic hobbies of mine with something computer related. Perhaps I could become a game designer or create a software tool that could help me and others with practicing artistic hobbies.
